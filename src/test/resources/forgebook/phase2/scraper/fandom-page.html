Diamond Pickaxe is crafted from three diamonds and two sticks arranged in a crafting table in the standard pickaxe pattern: three diamonds across the top row and two sticks in the middle and bottom of the center column. It is the most powerful standard pickaxe in vanilla Minecraft, capable of mining all types of ore including obsidian. The Diamond Pickaxe has a base durability of 1561 uses and can mine blocks at a speed rating of 8. It can be enchanted with Efficiency, Unbreaking, Fortune, Silk Touch, and Mending. Fortune III increases ore drops significantly: coal ore drops 1-2 coal (normally 1), diamond ore drops 1-4 diamonds (normally 1), and lapis lazuli ore drops 4-9 lapis (normally 1-6). Silk Touch allows mining of blocks that normally drop different items, such as obtaining the ore block itself instead of the dropped resource. The crafting recipe requires a workbench and cannot be crafted in the player inventory due to the 3x3 grid requirement. In survival mode, diamonds must be mined at Y-coordinate 16 or below.
